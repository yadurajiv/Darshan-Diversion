This game has been inspired by recent incidents of priests at Indian temples preventing women from entering, especially while menstruating, and hence diverting them away from their moment with god (darshan, as it's called in several Indian languages). Events are getting increasingly out of hand, as evidenced by this news article: http://theladiesfinger.com/temple-entry/ - and so what better way to demonstrate the utter insanity of the situation than make a game about it - especially given this year's theme.
